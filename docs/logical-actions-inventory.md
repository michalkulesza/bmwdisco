### Evidence limits

- An aggregate ID may have multiple providers; provider presence alone does not authorize broadcasting to them. The resolver selects one approved mapping or reports unknown/unsupported.
- The current direct semantic aliases are indicatorLeftAll → indicatorsLeft, indicatorRightAll → indicatorsRight, highBeamAll → bothHighBeams, lowBeamAll → bothLowBeams, and brakeAll → brakesAll.
- FRM3 indicator compositions and headlight candidates remain gated by their evidence and vehicle scope. A positive ECU response does not prove a visible lamp or successful release.
- A Demo response is simulation evidence and must not be promoted to physical mapping validation.
